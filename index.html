01 Form

A sphere that refuses to sit still.

Every vertex is pushed along its normal by layered simplex noise, then re-lit from its displaced neighbours — so the surface stays liquid-smooth however far it stretches.

02 Light

Colour from angle, not from paint.

There are no textures here. Hue is computed per pixel from how steeply the surface turns away from you, the way oil film and soap bubbles shift as you move around them.

03 Motion

Depth you can scroll through.

Scroll

The camera is the scrollbar

Each section is a keyframe. Position, scale, turbulence and hue ease between them as you move.

Pointer

Parallax that follows you

The whole scene leans gently toward your cursor, separating foreground from the star field.

Drag

Give it a push

Press and drag anywhere to throw the shape into a spin. It carries momentum, then settles.
